# Security policy

Rendra builds and runs code written by language models, so security reports are very welcome.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue. Report it privately instead, through this repository's **Security** tab, under **Report a vulnerability**. Include:
- what is affected;
- steps to reproduce;
- the impact you expect.

You will get a reply within a few days.

## Scope

Rendra is in active development and is not offered as a hosted service yet. Reports are most useful for:
- sandbox escape, or a generated app reaching the platform or another app;
- authentication, authorization or workspace-isolation flaws;
- secrets exposure, or injection into model prompts that leads to unsafe actions.

Never test against systems you do not own.
